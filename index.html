About & Safety

What PUJAPATH is

PUJAPATH answers one question: “I have a group, some time, and an idea of what I want — plan the entire Puja experience for me.”

Tell it in plain words — “we are 7 college friends, start 5 PM, South Kolkata, biryani somewhere, Garia by 11” — and it builds a realistic, chronological roadmap: start → pandal → small local puja → food → event → famous pandal → station → end.

The route engine weighs distance, travel time, visit durations, crowd levels, meal windows, group type and transport options. It fits as much as genuinely fits — never an arbitrary cap.

Honest data

This is a demo dataset compiled from widely published public knowledge. Coordinates, durations, crowd estimates and nearby services are approximate. Listings flagged demo are community-suggested. Nothing here replaces local verification — pandal timings, road closures and train schedules change during the festival.

Festival etiquette

· Bonedi baris are family homes — be quiet, dress modestly, ask before photographing.

· Anjali time (mornings, especially Ashtami) is for devotees — visit decoratively in the evenings.

· Keep your group's meeting point fixed before entering a famous pandal's crowd.

· Carry water, a power bank and some cash — many lanes and food rows are cash-only.

· On Dashami evening, avoid the ghats' approach roads unless you are going for bhashan.
